Palm Mile

We build GTA fail videos out of clips sent in by our community, and we pay out prizes for them every month.

What this is

Palm Mile is a Discord community around GTA V and GTA VI. Members send in their best fail clips. The ones we use go into our videos, credited with the name the sender chose.

Every month we pay out prizes: for the clips that made it into a video, for the people who brought others in, and in a draw that every clean post takes part in.

Taking part costs nothing. No purchase, no subscription, no payment at any point. The full rules, amounts and thresholds are published in the server and in the Terms of Participation.

How prizes are paid

  • Money is paid in US dollars by PayPal.
  • If you are 18 or older, you choose between money and Discord Nitro. Under 18, you receive Nitro, because PayPal needs an account of your own.
  • Every payout is listed publicly in our #hall-of-winners channel, with the amount and whether the winner confirmed that it arrived.

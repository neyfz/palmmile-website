What is Palm Mile

The best GTA moments, recorded by players.

Palm Mile builds GTA videos out of moments that players have recorded: the fails, the epic plays and everything in between.

Every clip carries the name of the player behind it. And in our Discord, the community runs monthly rewards for the people who send clips in.

How a video is made

From one clip to a Palm Mile video.

This is how Video 3 came together, from the first seconds to the name on screen. Scroll through it.

  1. Hook
  2. Intro
  3. The moments
  4. Name tag
  1. 01 / 04

    Hook

    The first seconds decide everything. Video 3 opens with some of its wildest moments, cut fast, before a single word is said.

  2. 02 / 04

    Intro

    Then a short intro: what the video is, who is behind it, and how your own clip can end up in the next one.

  3. 03 / 04

    The core: the clips, fails and epic plays, recorded by players across more than ten years of GTA. Each one picked, trimmed and put in order.

How it works

Three steps. No cost.

  1. 01

    Post your clip in #submit-a-clip on our Discord and tell us the name you want on screen.

  2. 02

    If we use it, your name goes into the video and into the credit list.

  3. 03

    Every month the community’s monthly rewards are paid out, in the open.

How it works in detail

Rewards

Monthly rewards, three ways in.

Everything is counted from what you post in our Discord. The current amounts and thresholds are posted in #how-it-works.

  • 01

    Clip leaderboard

    Every clip we accept counts here, in the month it is accepted, whether it ends up in a video or not.

  • 02

    Inviter leaderboard

    Bring people who send in clips. They count for you in the month their first clip is accepted.

  • 03

    Monthly draw

    Every clean post earns a ticket. At the end of the month the draw is decided by chance, from all tickets of that month.

And beyond the boards

  • When your clip is used

    You get a colour role, your clip gets a post in #featured that says where in the video it is, and your name goes into the credit list.

  • Month role

    Post regularly and the month role is yours. Once the prize pot is open, the draw hands out month roles as well.

  • Inviter roles

    Roles that show how many people you have brought into the community.

    PlugFixerKingpin

  • Hall of winners

    Every payout is listed in #hall-of-winners with the amount and whether it arrived. Your name appears there only if you choose to show it.

  • Money is paid in US dollars by PayPal.
  • If you are 18 or older, you choose between money and Discord Nitro. Under 18, you receive Nitro.
  • Winners are announced in the Discord and also told by direct message.

Taking part costs nothing.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is Palm Mile free?

Yes. Taking part costs nothing: no purchase, no subscription, no payment at any point.

Who records the clips?

Players. Every clip in a Palm Mile video was recorded by a player, and that player is named in the video and in the public credit list.

How do I send in a clip?

Join our Discord and post it in #submit-a-clip, together with the name you want on screen. The pinned example post shows how.

How do the monthly rewards work?

Three ways, every month: the clip leaderboard, the inviter leaderboard and the monthly draw. The current amounts and thresholds are in #how-it-works.

Where can I watch the videos?

On our YouTube channel. Short versions run on TikTok, Instagram and Snapchat.

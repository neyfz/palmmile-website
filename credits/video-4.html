TOP 500 EPIC MOMENTS YOU NEED TO WATCH BEFORE GTA 6

Runtime 1:05:27 · 500 moments · 431 creators

Every name in this video is the player who recorded that moment.

These clips come from creators across more than ten years of Grand Theft Auto V. The vast majority were filmed and first published by the players themselves on Medal.tv, on their own YouTube channels, on Reddit, in forums, on Discord servers, on social media and across countless GTA communities. Over the years they have been passed on, reposted and collected again and again, long before they reached us.

We credit what we were able to find, and we link what still works. The list below gives the creator of every clip and the timestamp where it appears. Some of these clips are more than ten years old. Sometimes the channel is gone, sometimes only a name survived, and sometimes a clip reached us with nothing attached to it at all. A line that says "no link" means exactly that: we could not find a working channel for that creator, and we would rather say so than guess.

If you are in this video and your credit is missing, wrong, or should point somewhere else, message us on Discord or write to copyright@palmmile.com and we will fix it. If you want your clip taken down, it will be removed the same day, no questions asked.

How it works

From your clip to the video.

Palm Mile builds long GTA videos out of moments that players record. Send in your clip and get your name in the video. In our Discord, the community also runs monthly rewards. Taking part costs nothing. This page walks you through all of it.

Start in the Discord

Everything at Palm Mile happens in our Discord: clips are sent in there, the monthly boards run there and every payout is listed there. Joining is free.

  1. 01Join the server with the invite link.
  2. 02Read #how-it-works. It holds the current amounts, thresholds and rules for the month, and it is part of the Terms of Participation.
  3. 03Open #submit-a-clip and look at the pinned example post before you post your own.
Join the Discord

Your clip

Post your clip in #submit-a-clip and tell us the name you want on screen. That name is how you are credited, in the video and in the credit list. You decide what it is.

  • Your own recording

    Send clips you recorded yourself. Claiming a clip you did not record is not allowed.

  • One moment, clearly shown

    The moment should be easy to see from start to end. We trim it for the video.

  • We review every clip

    Whether a clip is accepted, and whether it is used in a video, is decided by us.

  • Accepted counts

    Every accepted clip counts towards the clip leaderboard in the month it is accepted, used in a video or not.

When your clip is used

A used clip becomes one of the 500 moments in a Palm Mile video, with a name tag showing the name you chose.

  • In the video

    Your name tag appears on screen while your clip plays.

  • In the credit list

    Your name and the time of your clip go into the public credit list of that video.

  • In the Discord

    You get a colour role, and your clip gets a post in #featured that says where in the video it is.

Monthly rewards

  • 01

    Clip leaderboard

    Counts your accepted clips in the month they are accepted. Decided by performance, from the server’s own records.

  • 02

    Inviter leaderboard

    Counts the people you bring in, in the month their first clip is accepted.

  • 03

    Monthly draw

    Every clean post earns a ticket, up to a monthly maximum. The draw is decided by chance from all tickets of the month.

One money prize per person per month. Months are counted in German time. The prize pot, how it is split and the amounts are published in #how-it-works.

Roles

  • Month role

    Reach the posting threshold and the month role is yours. Once the pot is open, a share of the tickets in the draw wins it too.

  • Colour role

    For every member whose clip is used in a video.

  • Inviter roles

    Roles that show how many people you have brought into the community.

Getting paid

Winners are announced in #winners and also told by direct message. You claim your prize there.

  • Money

    Paid in US dollars by PayPal. We only ever ask for the email address of your PayPal account.

  • Discord Nitro

    If you are 18 or older, you choose between money and Nitro. Under 18, you receive Nitro.

  • Public proof

    Every payout is listed in #hall-of-winners with the amount and whether it arrived.

  • Your choice

    Your name appears in #hall-of-winners only if you choose to show it.

Read the Terms of Participation

Your name

We never ask for your real name. You choose the name we credit you as. If your credit is missing, wrong or should point somewhere else, message us on Discord or write to copyright@palmmile.com and we will fix it. If you want your clip taken down, it will be removed the same day.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Taking part costs nothing: no purchase, no subscription, no payment at any point. If anyone ever asks you to pay to enter, it is not us.

Who can win?

Anyone who is a member of the server can take part and win, except the people who run it. The details are in section 1 of the Terms of Participation.

How do I get paid?

Money is paid by PayPal, in US dollars. Nitro comes as a gift link. How a prize is claimed is described in section 5 of the Terms of Participation.

I am under 18.

You can take part in everything and win everything. Under 18 you receive Discord Nitro instead of money, because PayPal needs an account of your own. See section 6 of the Terms of Participation.

What are the current amounts and thresholds?

They are published in the #how-it-works channel in the Discord, and that channel is part of the Terms of Participation. They can change, so they are not repeated here.

Does my clip have to be used in a video to count?

No. Every accepted clip counts towards the clip leaderboard in the month it is accepted.

Can I change the name I am credited under?

Yes. Message us on Discord or write to copyright@palmmile.com.

My clip is in a video but my credit is wrong.

Message us on Discord or write to copyright@palmmile.com and we will fix it. If you want your clip taken down, it will be removed the same day, no questions asked.
